Our Mission

The missing link
in India's
chip stack

Every chip fabbed in India starts with a wafer — yet not a single domestic facility slices silicon wafers at scale. JSK Technologies exists to close that gap.

Anchored to India's Semiconductor Mission and strategically located in Gujarat, we design a precision wafering operation supplying both semiconductor fabs and solar cell manufacturers.

This is not an assembly play. This is deep-tech substrate manufacturing — done in India, owned by India.

What We Build

Precision at every micron

⬡

Silicon Ingot Slicing

Wire saw technology for high-precision CZ and FZ silicon ingot cutting. Sub-micron thickness control enables consistent wafer geometry across full production runs.

Core Process
◎

Semiconductor Grade

150mm → 200mm → 300mm progressive roadmap for CMOS, power devices, and compound semiconductors. Full SEMI M1 spec compliance. We start proven, then scale.

Primary Market
◈

Solar Grade Wafers

Monocrystalline M2–M12 formats for next-gen TOPCon and HJT solar cells. Positioned to capture ALMM-III domestic content mandates from 2028.

ALMM-III 2028
▣

Surface Preparation

Lapping, chemical etching, polishing, and edge grinding in an integrated cleanroom — delivering RMS roughness below 0.1 nm for device-ready wafers.

Finishing
⊕

Quality & Metrology

Inline LPD inspection, TTV measurement, and SIMS analysis. Every wafer is fully characterized before dispatch — zero-defect culture from Day 1.

QA/QC
⟳

Kerf Recovery

Proprietary slurry recovery and silicon kerf reclaim reduces material waste and operating cost — a sustainability advantage in a cost-sensitive market.
